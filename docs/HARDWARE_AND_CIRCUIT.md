# Hardware Specifications & Circuit Diagrams (MECH-MINDS)
- **Edge Compute**: Raspberry Pi 4 Model B (8GB)
- **Vision Sensor**: Arducam IMX219 Wide-Angle Camera
- **Motor Driver**: L298N Dual H-Bridge Motor Driver
- **Actuators**: 4x 12V 300RPM DC Geared Motors
- **IMU**: MPU6050 6-Axis Gyro/Accelerometer
- **Power**: 3S 11.1V 5200mAh LiPo + XL4015 Buck Converter
